PRESS RELEASE · 8K10Q.com

Theriva (TOVX): Board Approved the Split Before the Vote. Shareholders Learned After.

The reverse split has not happened yet. It takes effect the night Theriva’s answer to a shareholder lawsuit is due.

November 23, 2026 – Mark Nejmeh, a Theriva Biologics (NYSE American: TOVX) stockholder suing the company and four of its directors, today asked the U.S. District Court for the District of Nevada for leave to add new events to his case (Nejmeh v. Theriva Biologics, Inc., No. 3:26-cv-00705, ECF No. 58).

Theriva’s own filings show its Board of Directors authorized a 1-for-30 reverse stock split on November 13, 2026. Theriva filed nothing with the SEC about that decision before stockholders voted on November 18, and it announced the split on November 20. The split has not happened yet. It takes effect at 11:59 p.m. Eastern on December 1, 2026, the same day Theriva’s answer to the complaint is due; shares trade split-adjusted from December 2. Mr. Nejmeh believes the split was timed to fall the day after Theriva answers.

Trading halted: at 10:47 a.m. Eastern on November 23, NYSE American halted trading in TOVX, listing the reason as “Regulatory Concern.” As of 2:17 p.m. Eastern, no resume time had been posted and Theriva had filed nothing with the SEC about it.

The new filing adds a claim that Theriva did not correct its proxy materials after the Board’s November 13 decision. It asks for 60 days’ notice to stockholders before any sale, license or merger of the company or its drug candidate VCN-01.

“The reverse split proves that the warrant meeting was not moot,” said Mr. Nejmeh, founder of 8K10Q.com.

The claims are allegations; no court has ruled on them. Theriva’s response to the new motion is due December 7, 2026. Every filing Mr. Nejmeh makes is posted at nejmehvstheriva.com, and Theriva’s own SEC filings can be read in plain English on 8K10Q.com.
