Re: Theriva Biologics, Inc. (NYSE American: TOVX) – trading and disclosure, November 2026

I am a stockholder of Theriva Biologics, Inc. I ask the Commission to review the following events, each taken from Theriva’s own SEC filings or the public court record:

  1. On November 6, 2026, Theriva’s Chief Executive Officer declared under penalty of perjury that “[a]s of the market close on November 5, 2026, Theriva’s common stock trades at $0.17 per share, well below the $0.54 per share exercise price of the New Warrants.” (Nejmeh v. Theriva Biologics, Inc., No. 3:26-cv-00705 (D. Nev.), ECF No. 50-1 ¶ 8.)
  2. On November 13, 2026, Theriva’s Board of Directors authorized a 1-for-30 reverse stock split. (Form 8-K filed November 20, 2026, Exhibit 99.1, Accession No. 0001104659-26-130644.)
  3. Theriva filed nothing with the SEC from November 13 through November 17. On November 18, its stockholders voted at a special meeting on an increase in authorized shares and on the shares issuable under its October 2025 warrants. (Form 8-K filed November 18, 2026, Accession No. 0001104659-26-129752.)
  4. Theriva announced the split in a Form 8-K accepted by the SEC at 4:35 p.m. Eastern on November 20, 2026, after the market closed. The split takes effect at 11:59 p.m. Eastern on December 1, 2026. (Accession No. 0001104659-26-130644.)
  5. In its annual report filed March 12, 2026, Theriva wrote that “the NYSE American views trading at or below a price of $0.10 to be abnormally low” and that the exchange can “immediately suspend trading” in that event. (Form 10-K, Accession No. 0001104659-26-026767.)
  6. On November 23, 2026, NYSE American halted trading at 10:47 a.m. Eastern. Theriva reported that the halt was “due to the low trading price of the Company’s shares of Common Stock for a substantial period of time and the fact that its Common Stock traded below $0.10 on November 23, 2026,” and that it expects the halt to last until the split. (Form 8-K filed November 23, 2026, Accession No. 0001104659-26-131095.)

I ask the Commission to review the trading in TOVX from November 13 through November 23, 2026, including who bought and sold before the split was announced, and whether stockholders should have been told of the November 13 decision before they voted on November 18.

Thank you.
