Write to the SEC about Theriva (TOVX)

If you hold Theriva stock and want the SEC to look at what happened in November 2026, this page builds a letter for you. The facts in it come from Theriva’s own SEC filings and the court record, with sources. You add your name and what you saw yourself, then print, sign and mail it.

SEC Office of the Whistleblower (c/o ENF-CPU) 1604 Springhill Road, Suite 110 Vienna, VA 22182 ATTN: SEC TCR SUBMISSIONS

Re: Theriva Biologics, Inc. (NYSE American: TOVX), SEC Central Index Key 0000894158; Nejmeh v. Theriva Biologics, Inc., No. 3:26-cv-00705-ART-CSD (D. Nev.)

I am a stockholder of Theriva Biologics, Inc. I ask the Commission to review the following events, each taken from Theriva’s own SEC filings or the public court record. They are also before the federal court in Nejmeh v. Theriva Biologics, Inc., No. 3:26-cv-00705-ART-CSD (D. Nev.), a securities case brought by another stockholder and filed September 16, 2026.

  1. On October 16–17, 2025, Theriva cut the exercise price of outstanding warrants to $0.54 and gave their holders 16,184,560 new warrants, two for each one exercised. (Form 8-K filed October 17, 2025, Accession No. 0001104659-25-100468.)
  2. On August 3, 2026, stockholders approved the shares for those new warrants at a meeting whose quorum depended on 10,823,826 broker shares voted without instructions. (Form 8-K filed August 4, 2026, Accession No. 0001104659-26-090064.) Theriva later stated that its proxy statement for that meeting contained errors. (Preliminary Proxy Statement filed October 9, 2026, Accession No. 0001104659-26-115074.)
  3. On November 2, 2026, Theriva reported the sale of its SYN-004, SYN-007 and certain SYN-006 assets to Biossil for $350,000 upfront. (Form 8-K, Accession No. 0001104659-26-121318.)
  4. On November 6, 2026, in a sworn declaration filed in the court case above, Theriva’s Chief Executive Officer stated that its stock closed at $0.17 on November 5, 2026, below the $0.54 exercise price of the new warrants. (ECF No. 50-1 ¶ 8.)
  5. On November 13, 2026, Theriva’s Board of Directors authorized a 1-for-30 reverse stock split. (Form 8-K filed November 20, 2026, Exhibit 99.1, Accession No. 0001104659-26-130644.)
  6. Theriva filed nothing with the SEC from November 13 through November 17. On November 18, its stockholders voted at a special meeting on an increase in authorized shares and on the shares issuable under its October 2025 warrants. (Form 8-K filed November 18, 2026, Accession No. 0001104659-26-129752.)
  7. Theriva announced the split in a Form 8-K accepted by the SEC at 4:35 p.m. Eastern on November 20, 2026, after the market closed. The split takes effect at 11:59 p.m. Eastern on December 1, 2026. (Accession No. 0001104659-26-130644.)
  8. In its annual report filed March 12, 2026, Theriva wrote that “the NYSE American views trading at or below a price of $0.10 to be abnormally low” and that the exchange can “immediately suspend trading” in that event. (Form 10-K, Accession No. 0001104659-26-026767.)
  9. On November 23, 2026, NYSE American halted trading at 10:47 a.m. Eastern. Theriva reported that the halt was “due to the low trading price of the Company’s shares of Common Stock for a substantial period of time and the fact that its Common Stock traded below $0.10 on November 23, 2026,” and that it expects the halt to last until the split. (Form 8-K filed November 23, 2026, Accession No. 0001104659-26-131095.)

I ask the Commission to review the trading in TOVX from November 13 through November 23, 2026, including who bought and sold before the split was announced, and whether stockholders should have been told of the November 13 decision before they voted on November 18.

Thank you.
