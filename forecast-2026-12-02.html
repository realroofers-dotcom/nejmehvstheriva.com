What Mark Nejmeh, the plaintiff, expects to happen to Theriva Biologics (NYSE American: TOVX) stockholders, and what Theriva’s own filings allow. Each forecast is labelled as his belief; each fact beside it gives its source. This is his opinion, not investment advice, and no court has ruled on it.

Attention shareholders:

I think the halt was orchestrated by the company. I think the posts that suddenly appeared on Stocktwits blaming the lawsuit are there to convince you to blame the lawsuit. Source for the figure: Theriva’s Form 10-Q filed November 12, 2026 (accumulated deficit at September 30, 2026; “Since inception, the Company has financed its activities principally from the proceeds of the issuance of equity securities”).

The forecast, step by step

1. Now: the halt

FactNYSE American halted trading in TOVX at 10:47 a.m. Eastern on November 23, 2026. Theriva’s Form 8-K filed at 4:30 p.m. that day says the halt was due to the low trading price and trading below $0.10, and that it expects the halt to remain until the reverse split. Theriva’s own annual report of March 12, 2026 says NYSE American “views trading at or below a price of $0.10 to be abnormally low.”

FactWhile trading is halted, no one can buy and no one can sell.

He thinksThe halt was orchestrated by the company. Who asked for it, and what notice the exchange gave, has not been made public; the plaintiff asked Theriva on November 23, and it had not answered when it filed its motion for more time that evening (ECF No. 62-4 ¶ 5).

2. Now: the posts blaming the lawsuit

FactFrom September 16, when the case was filed, through November 22, no post on the public $TOVX stream on Stocktwits blamed the lawsuit. The first one appeared at 12:48 p.m. Eastern on November 23, about two hours after the halt and before Theriva’s 8-K gave any reason for it, from an account created that same day, with no followers, whose only posts are about the lawsuit. In September, a stockholder posting about the lawsuit wrote that his posts were not being published.

He thinksThese posts are there to convince stockholders to blame the lawsuit instead of the company.

3. December 1, 11:59 p.m. Eastern: the 1-for-30 reverse split

FactThe board authorized the split on November 13, 2026, before the November 18 vote, and announced it on November 20 (Form 8-K, November 20, 2026). About 1,529,756 shares will be outstanding after it (ECF No. 63-1, Appendix A).

FactThe 500,000,000 authorized shares approved on November 18 are not adjusted for the split: about 327 times the shares then outstanding (ECF No. 63-1).

4. December 2: trading reopens, if the exchange allows it

FactTheriva says split-adjusted trading is to begin December 2, “subject to NYSE American approval” (Form 8-K, November 23, 2026).

Fact: what happened after the last splitFour days after the August 26, 2024 split, 26,144,100 TOVX shares traded, about twenty-six times the shares outstanding; the price reached $7.15 and closed at $5.90. A month later Theriva priced an offering through A.G.P. at $1.75, and on the day it closed the stock closed at $1.49 (ECF No. 54 ¶ 34).

Fact: May 7, 2025Theriva released trial results and priced an offering the same day; 2,803,100 shares were reported sold short and the stock fell from $1.36 to $0.81 (ECF No. 54 ¶¶ 50–52).

He thinksAn announcement on or about December 2 will run the price up, and the stock will then be shorted and fall.

5. The warrants

FactThe 16,184,560 New Warrants at $0.54 become about 539,486 warrants after the split, about 35% of the shares then outstanding (ECF No. 63-1). By the plaintiff’s calculation their exercise price becomes about $16.20 ($0.54 × 30).

FactWith a holder’s consent, Theriva may “voluntarily reduce the then current exercise price to any amount and for any period of time” (Theriva’s October 2026 proxy statement; ECF No. 54 ¶ 86). In any merger or reverse merger, the holders may require the successor to buy their unexercised warrants for cash (ECF No. 54 ¶ 55).

Fact: October 2025Theriva cut the price of earlier warrants to $0.54 and gave their holders two new warrants for each one exercised. Stockholders first heard of it on October 16, 2025, after the agreement had already been made (ECF No. 54 ¶ 55).

He thinksThe warrants will be exercised at a price stockholders are not told in advance.

6. The reverse merger

FactTheriva’s Form 10-Q says it has been in discussions about strategic alternatives that “may include a business combination, merger or reverse merger.” On July 24, 2026 the board made the directors’ and employees’ options vest when a definitive merger agreement is signed. On August 10, 2026 Theriva rewrote its chief executive’s agreement to pay out on signing a reverse-merger agreement, in terms that capture “all reverse merger structures” (ECF No. 54 ¶¶ 88–89).

FactTheriva has sold its SYN-004, SYN-007 and certain SYN-006 assets for $350,000 upfront (Form 8-K, November 2, 2026) and licensed SYN-020 for $300,000 upfront (Form 8-K, February 18, 2026).

He thinksThe warrants will make the company disappear into a reverse merger, leaving today’s stockholders with a small fraction of the company, as his complaint alleges (ECF No. 54 ¶ 102). He expects great loss for other shareholders.

What stockholders can watch for

  • Any Form 8-K or press release from Theriva on or after December 1.
  • Any notice that the New Warrants’ exercise price has been reduced, or that New Warrants have been exercised.
  • A jump in the number of shares outstanding in a prospectus supplement or quarterly report.
  • Any merger agreement or letter of intent, and Forms 4 from the directors when their options vest.
