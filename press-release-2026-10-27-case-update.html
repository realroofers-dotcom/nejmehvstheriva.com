CASE UPDATE · October 27, 2026

The case continues. On October 26 the Court found that Mr. Nejmeh "appears to be indigent, at least to the extent that he would be unable to pay hefty PACER fees likely to be associated with this case." It granted him free access to the case records and denied his request to post them on his website (ECF No. 44). The full record is public on PACER, No. 3:26-cv-00705-ART-CSD (D. Nev.).

Next: Theriva's response to the motion to postpone its November 18 meeting in Spain is due November 5. Its response to the complaint is due December 1.
